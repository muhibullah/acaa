CO-CREATED IN EVERY COUNTRY

Children shape the world they learn in

Workshops with children explore language, daily life and national curricula. With trauma-aware researchers, they also test which colours, objects, sounds and symbols feel safe and which carry difficult associations. The result is one game engine with a different visual world for every country.
